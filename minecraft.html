minecraft art

For my object of cultural significance, I chose a fish skeleton. In the Philippines, fish are a staple part of the Filipino diet as it's easily attainable and affordable. Growing up, my grandma always cooked meals that included fish, even doing the extra steps to remove all the tinik, or small fish bones, for baby Melusine. Thus, I wanted to create an object that was familiar in my household growing up. Also, I just think fish skeletons are cool and wanted to make one. The fish motif is one that frequently pops up in art I make.
